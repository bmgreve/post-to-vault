Support

Post is a quick way to get a note into your vault: open it, write, send, and it lands in today's daily note.

Setting up

On first launch Post asks where your vault is kept and asks you to pick its folder. It reads how your daily notes are named from your vault's settings, and sends a first note so you can see where notes will go. You can change where notes go later in Settings.

A note didn't arrive

If your vault can't be reached, notes wait safely on your phone and file themselves when it can be. Settings shows anything still waiting.
